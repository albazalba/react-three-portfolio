# Model sources

These files come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models) and are CC0 (public domain, no credit required).
Each was packed into one GLB with its textures resized to 512 px and converted to WebP.

| File | Poly Haven asset |
| --- | --- |
| `potted_plant_02.glb` | Potted Plant 02 |
| `potted_plant_04.glb` | Potted Plant 04 |
| `gaming_console.glb` | Gaming Console |
| `gamepad.glb` | Gamepad |
| `boombox.glb` | Boombox |
| `ceramic_vase_01.glb` | Ceramic Vase 01 |

The two cars in the game room's miniature collection come from the [Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets):

| File | Asset | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `concept_car.glb` | [Car Concept](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept) by Eric Chadwick, © 2024 Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `toy_car.glb` | [Toy Car](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ToyCar) by Guido Odendahl and Eric Chadwick | CC0 |

Both were changed: meshes simplified, hidden parts (wipers, pedals, brake pads, seat frames) removed, textures resized to 512 px WebP, and the material variants dropped. The Khronos and 3D Commerce logos, which their licence excludes, were removed from the concept car (badge texture, tyre sidewalls and tread). The page repaints both cars in different colours. The CC BY credit is also shown on the collection's wall plaque.

`bench.glb`, `pedestal.glb`, `planter.glb` and `frame.glb` were already in the project and are not from Poly Haven.

The brand marks on the tech stack board are not models: they are SVG paths from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.38.1 (CC0-1.0), kept in `components/museum/stackIcons.ts`.
